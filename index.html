15 . COSMIC DETECTIVE

Evidence first. Conclusions second.

Practice the habits of scientific interpretation: separate the observation from what you infer, and ask what additional data would reduce uncertainty.

Practice case

Case 01 · A change in brightness

Research habit

Ask what would test it.

A responsible next step is to compare calibrated observations, check instrument settings, inspect uncertainty and background effects, and seek an independent measurement when possible.

A difference in an image is a starting question—not proof of a new planet, life, or other discovery.
