01 / The mission

The universe changes.
How do we notice?

Andromeda is a concept for making astronomical observations easier to explore and compare. Its goal is to help people ask better questions about what they see in the sky.

04 / Our principles

Curiosity needs evidence.

A useful space-science tool should make its limits as clear as its possibilities.

Evidence before claims

Potential changes are candidates for investigation, not automatic discoveries.

Clear data sources

Real observations should identify their source, instrument, and relevant metadata.

Account for uncertainty

Noise, alignment, calibration, and observing conditions can affect comparisons.

Make science accessible

Explain what the tool shows, what it does not show, and what needs further review.
