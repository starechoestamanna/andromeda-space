04 / SPHEREx investigation lab

One sky. Different wavelengths.

SPHEREx observes infrared light, which is invisible to human eyes. Scientists can represent different infrared wavelengths with visible colours to study different properties of astronomical objects.

3.29 µm · DUST
0.98 µm · OTHER VIEW

Image credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech. The two views show the same region at different infrared wavelengths. The dust feature visible at 3.29 microns is not visible in the 0.98-micron view.

Mission overview

What is SPHEREx?

SPHEREx is a NASA space telescope designed to survey the entire sky in 102 infrared spectral bands. Its observations help researchers investigate galaxies, the early universe, and ingredients in interstellar clouds.

Spectral bands 102

05 / About ANDROMEDA

Explore carefully. Ask better questions.

ANDROMEDA brings interactive astronomy learning and public scientific imagery into one place. The goal is to make space exploration approachable while treating scientific evidence honestly.

Evidence

Sources matter

Real images link to their original source pages. Check the credits, context and limitations before drawing conclusions.

Uncertainty

Compare, then investigate

Differences can come from wavelength, processing, alignment, instrument effects or real astronomical changes. Further checks are needed before claiming a discovery.
